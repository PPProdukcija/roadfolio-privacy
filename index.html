Privacy Policy for RoadFolio

Last updated: August 23, 2026

RoadFolio (also listed as "RoadFolio — Vehicle Passport") is a personal vehicle service-book app made by PPProdukcija. This page explains, plainly and completely, what data the app handles and why. RoadFolio has no account system and no servers of its own — almost everything it does happens entirely on your iPhone.

Data you create

The vehicles, service records, mileage history, photos, and documents you add in RoadFolio are stored locally on your device only. This build of RoadFolio does not sync to iCloud or anywhere else — your logbook stays on this iPhone and in your own device backups. Use the app's "Export everything" option if you want a copy of your own data.

Camera and photos

With your permission, RoadFolio uses the camera to scan a VIN, read your dashboard's mileage, and capture service invoices, and can attach photos or documents from your photo library to a vehicle record. All of this scanning and text recognition happens on your device using Apple's built-in Vision framework — no photo, document, or camera frame is ever uploaded anywhere.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you choose to dictate a record instead of typing it, RoadFolio uses on-device speech recognition only (`requiresOnDeviceRecognition` is enforced in code) — your voice is never sent off the device, and if this iPhone can't recognize speech offline, dictation simply doesn't happen.

VIN online lookup (optional, off by default)

A VIN decoded on your device can only reveal the manufacturer and model year — that's genuinely all a VIN encodes offline. If you turn on "VIN online lookup" in Settings, RoadFolio will additionally send the scanned VIN to NHTSA's free, public vPIC vehicle database (a U.S. government service, vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov) to try to add the model name and a few other details. This is the only network request RoadFolio ever makes, anywhere in the app:

  • It is off by default — you have to turn it on yourself.
  • Only the VIN itself is sent. Nothing else — no name, account, location, or device identifier — is attached to that request.
  • It only happens when you scan or enter a VIN, and only while the setting is on.
  • NHTSA is a public government lookup service, not an analytics, advertising, or tracking partner, and RoadFolio does not use the VIN or the response for anything besides showing you the extra vehicle detail.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, RoadFolio uses them to remind you about upcoming vehicle maintenance. Notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device.

Purchases

RoadFolio offers optional monthly, yearly, and lifetime unlocks through Apple's App Store (StoreKit). Payment is handled entirely by Apple — RoadFolio never sees or stores your payment details, and has no server of its own to send purchase data to. RoadFolio only asks your iPhone, via Apple's StoreKit APIs, whether an entitlement is active, so it can unlock the corresponding features.

What we don't do

  • We don't run analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • We don't collect your name, email, or contacts — RoadFolio has no account system at all.
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties. The one exception — sending a scanned VIN to NHTSA's public database — is described above in full, is opt-in, and is off by default.
  • We don't track you or your device across other companies' apps or websites.

Children's privacy

RoadFolio is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.
